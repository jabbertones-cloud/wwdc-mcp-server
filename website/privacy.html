AppDev Atlas (the service at wwdcmcp.smatdesigns.com and the public MCP endpoint at wwdc-mcp.smatdesigns.com/mcp) is a read-only Apple developer knowledge tool operated by SMAT Designs. It helps coding agents retrieve public WWDC, Apple documentation, HIG, Swift Evolution, and App Review guideline evidence.

Developed independently by SMAT Designs; not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple or OpenAI.

Information processed

When you use the hosted MCP endpoint or ChatGPT plugin, we process:

  • MCP protocol requests (tool names, arguments you send, and tool results returned)
  • standard HTTP/connection metadata (IP address, user agent, timestamps) via our host Cloudflare
  • optional client identifiers supplied by ChatGPT/OpenAI for the plugin session

Do not send passwords, API keys, private certificates, or unrelated personal data in prompts or tool arguments. Your AI provider (for example OpenAI) also processes chat content under its own privacy terms.

What we do not collect

  • Apple Developer or App Store Connect account credentials
  • payment card data through this service
  • write access to your private repositories or Apple account

The service indexes and returns public Apple/Swift source material. It does not require an Apple login.

Service providers

We use Cloudflare to host the website and MCP edge. OpenAI may connect as a ChatGPT plugin client. Apple content remains subject to Apple’s terms. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and choices

We do not maintain a separate, persistent history of individual MCP tool arguments or responses. Cloudflare may retain request metadata and diagnostic logs for security, abuse prevention, and reliability for up to seven days (shorter on some plans). These operational logs expire under our hosting provider's retention policy. Aggregated service metrics that do not identify individual requesters may be retained longer.

You can stop sending data to AppDev Atlas by disconnecting the plugin or stopping MCP requests. For privacy access or deletion requests concerning data under our control, contact privacy@smatdesigns.com. Your AI client may retain its own chat history under its separate policy.
