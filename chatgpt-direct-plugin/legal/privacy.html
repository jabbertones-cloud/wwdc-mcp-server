WWDC MCP · PRIVACY · UPDATED OCTOBER 29, 2026

Your questions stay questions. We keep no profile of you.

WWDC MCP is operated by SMAT Designs (Scott Manthey) ("WWDC MCP," "we," "us"). This policy describes what the hosted WWDC MCP service at wwdc-mcp.smatdesigns.com does with information when you use it. WWDC MCP is a read-only search and reference service over public Apple developer material.

No accounts, no sign-in, no sale

The public service has no accounts and no sign-up. You do not log in, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or Apple ID to use it. WWDC MCP is free to use: we do not sell anything through this service and we do not process payments or collect payment details.

Information processed

When your AI client calls the service, we receive the MCP request: the tool name and the arguments you sent, such as a search query, a documentation path, or text you asked a tool to analyze. Requests are processed to produce the answer and are size-bounded. Do not send passwords, private keys, signing certificates, Apple account credentials, or other sensitive information in tool arguments — the service never needs them.

Tool answers come from an on-device index of public Apple material. The service does not save your queries or the content you submit into a database, and it keeps no per-user history.

Rate limiting uses a connection address, in memory only

To keep the public service fair and to discourage bulk extraction, tool calls are rate-limited by a token bucket keyed on a network address associated with your connection. That key and its token count exist only in the server's memory: they are never written to a database or disk, they are discarded when no longer needed or when the server restarts, and the number of keys held is capped. A rate-limited request receives only a generic "rate_limited" response and a Retry-After time.

Operational logs and analytics

The server records brief operational error messages (for example, that a request failed or the service restarted to load a refreshed index) so we can keep it running. It does not log your search queries as an analytics feed, it contains no advertising or product-analytics trackers, and it sets no cookies.

Hosting provider

The service is delivered through Cloudflare. Like any hosting provider, Cloudflare processes connection information (such as the IP address a request arrives from) in order to route, deliver, and protect the service, under Cloudflare's own privacy terms. We use Cloudflare's operational observability for the hosting layer itself — availability and error signals for the service, not profiles of individual users.

One tool fetches live from Apple

Most tools answer from our local index. One tool, apple_doc_lookup, fetches the documentation page you asked for directly from developer.apple.com so the answer is current; when you use it, the documentation path you requested is sent to Apple's servers, and Apple's own privacy policy applies to that request. No account information is included.

Your AI client

WWDC MCP answers the AI client you connected with (for example, ChatGPT). That client receives your prompts and the tool results, and handles them under its own privacy terms, which we do not control.

The corpus is public Apple material

Our index is built from public sources: WWDC sessions, Apple Developer Documentation, tutorials, the Human Interface Guidelines, Swift Evolution proposals, The Swift Programming Language, and the App Store Review Guidelines. It contains no private user data.

Retention and your choices

Because we keep no accounts and no database of your requests, there is no user profile to export or delete. Rate-limit state clears itself from memory as described above. If you have a privacy question or concern, contact us below and we will respond.
