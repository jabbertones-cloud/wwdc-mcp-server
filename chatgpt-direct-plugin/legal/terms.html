What WWDC MCP does

WWDC MCP is a read-only research service. It indexes public Apple developer material — WWDC20 through WWDC26 sessions and transcripts, Apple Developer Documentation, tutorials, the Human Interface Guidelines, Swift Evolution proposals, The Swift Programming Language, and the App Store Review Guidelines — and exposes search, lookup, comparison, and audit tools over that index to AI clients.
